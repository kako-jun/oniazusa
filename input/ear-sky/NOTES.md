# Ear-Sky Input Notes

Use the committed night background images from `ear-sky/public/bg/` as additional real-world
test inputs.

## Why these are useful

- strong dark/light contrast
- neon pink / cyan lighting
- blur and bokeh already present in the source
- night atmosphere that stresses tone reduction and tint decisions

## What to evaluate

- whether 3-tone reduction keeps the mood instead of flattening everything
- whether ordered dithering reads as intentional structure rather than noise
- whether left/right edge atmosphere survives the reduction
- whether neon areas become too harsh or too muddy
